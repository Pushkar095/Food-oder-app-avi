Shipping Policy

Fast, reliable delivery to your doorstep

Last Updated: December 31, 2024

Average Delivery Time: 30-45 minutes | Free Delivery: Orders over $30

1. Delivery Areas

Gourmet Go currently delivers to the following areas:

  • Within a 10-mile radius of our partner restaurants
  • All major neighborhoods in Foodie City and surrounding areas
  • Selected suburban locations (check availability at checkout)

Enter your delivery address during checkout to confirm if we deliver to your location. We're constantly expanding our service areas, so check back regularly if we don't currently serve your area.

2. Delivery Times

Our standard delivery hours are:

  • Monday - Thursday: 10:00 AM - 11:00 PM
  • Friday - Saturday: 10:00 AM - 12:00 AM (Midnight)
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM - 11:00 PM

Please note that individual restaurant hours may vary. Some restaurants may close earlier or have limited hours on certain days.

3. Estimated Delivery Time

The estimated delivery time displayed at checkout is calculated based on:

  • Current restaurant preparation time
  • Distance from restaurant to your location
  • Current traffic conditions
  • Number of active orders
  • Weather conditions

While we strive to meet estimated delivery times, they are approximate and not guaranteed. Delays may occur due to unforeseen circumstances.

4. Delivery Fees

Our delivery fee structure:

  • Standard Delivery: $3.99 - $6.99 (based on distance)
  • Free Delivery: Available on orders over $30
  • Express Delivery: Additional $4.99 for priority delivery
  • Peak Hours Surcharge: May apply during high-demand periods (Friday/Saturday evenings)

The exact delivery fee will be displayed before you complete your order.

5. Order Tracking

Track your order in real-time:

  • Receive SMS notifications at each stage
  • Track your delivery driver's location on the map
  • Get estimated arrival time updates
  • Contact your driver directly if needed

6. Delivery Instructions

You can provide special delivery instructions including:

  • Gate codes or building access information
  • Preferred drop-off location
  • Contactless delivery preferences
  • Specific parking instructions

Please ensure someone is available to receive the order at the delivery address.

7. Contactless Delivery

For your safety and convenience, we offer contactless delivery:

  • Driver will place food at your door
  • You'll receive a notification when order arrives
  • Driver will maintain safe distance
  • Photo confirmation of delivery available

8. Failed Delivery Attempts

If our driver cannot complete delivery due to:

  • No one available to receive the order
  • Incorrect or incomplete address
  • Unsafe delivery conditions
  • Customer unreachable by phone

The driver will attempt to contact you. If unsuccessful after 10 minutes, the order may be cancelled without refund. A delivery attempt fee may apply.

9. Temperature Control

We maintain food quality through:

  • Insulated thermal bags for all deliveries
  • Separate hot and cold compartments
  • Temperature monitoring during transit
  • Sealed packaging to prevent contamination

10. Delivery During Severe Weather

During severe weather conditions:

  • Delivery times may be extended
  • Some areas may become temporarily unavailable
  • We prioritize driver safety above all
  • You'll be notified of any significant delays

You can cancel your order for a full refund if weather causes excessive delays.

11. Large Orders and Catering

For orders over $200 or catering requests:

  • Please place orders at least 24 hours in advance
  • Contact our catering team at catering@gourmetgo.com
  • Special delivery arrangements can be made
  • Dedicated delivery personnel for large orders

12. Incorrect Address

If you provide an incorrect delivery address:

  • Contact us immediately after placing the order
  • We'll attempt to update the address if the order hasn't been dispatched
  • Additional delivery fees may apply for address changes
  • Once the driver is en route, address changes may not be possible

13. Missing Items or Issues

If you receive an incorrect or incomplete order:

  • Contact customer support immediately
  • Provide your order number and details of the issue
  • We'll arrange a replacement or refund
  • Take photos of the issue for faster resolution

14. Tipping Your Driver

Tips are optional but greatly appreciated:

  • 100% of tips go directly to your driver
  • You can add a tip during checkout or after delivery
  • Cash tips are also welcome
  • Suggested tip: 15-20% of order total

15. Eco-Friendly Delivery

We're committed to sustainability:

  • Zero-emission electric vehicle fleet in select areas
  • Recyclable and biodegradable packaging
  • Route optimization to reduce carbon footprint
  • Reusable container program (coming soon)

16. Delivery Partner Program

Interested in becoming a delivery driver? We offer:

  • Flexible scheduling
  • Competitive pay plus tips
  • Insurance coverage
  • Weekly payouts

Apply at careers@gourmetgo.com

17. Contact Delivery Support

For delivery-related questions or issues:

  • Email: delivery@gourmetgo.com
  • Phone: +1 (555) 123-4567
  • Live Chat: Available on our website 24/7
  • In-App Support: Message us directly through the app

18. Policy Updates

We may update this Shipping Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our delivery practices or service areas. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when the policy was last revised.
