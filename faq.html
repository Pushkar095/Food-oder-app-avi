Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our service

Ordering & Delivery

How do I place an order?
Simply browse our menu, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can create an account or checkout as a guest. Enter your delivery address and payment details to complete your order.
What are your delivery hours?
We deliver from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, seven days a week. Some partner restaurants may have different operating hours.
How long does delivery take?
Our average delivery time is 30-45 minutes. You'll receive real-time updates on your order status through our app and SMS notifications.
What is your delivery area?
We currently deliver within a 10-mile radius of our partner restaurants. Enter your address at checkout to confirm delivery availability.

Payment & Pricing

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Cash on delivery is also available in select areas.
Are there any delivery fees?
Delivery fees vary based on distance and order size. Orders over $30 qualify for free delivery. You'll see the exact delivery fee before confirming your order.
Do you offer discounts or promotions?
Yes! We regularly offer promotional codes, first-time user discounts, and loyalty rewards. Subscribe to our newsletter to stay updated on the latest deals.

Food Quality & Safety

How do you ensure food quality?
We partner only with certified restaurants and use advanced thermal packaging to maintain optimal temperature. Our delivery fleet follows strict hygiene protocols.
Can I customize my order?
Absolutely! You can add special instructions for each item, request ingredient modifications, and specify dietary preferences during checkout.
What if I have food allergies?
Each menu item includes detailed ingredient information. You can also add allergy notes to your order, and we'll communicate them directly to the restaurant.

Returns & Support

What if my order is incorrect or damaged?
Contact our support team immediately through the app or website. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund within 24 hours.
Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled within 5 minutes of placement for a full refund. After preparation begins, cancellations may incur a fee.
How do I contact customer support?
Reach us via email at hello@gourmetgo.com, call +1 (555) 123-4567, or use the live chat feature on our website. We're available 24/7.
